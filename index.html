Food

Know what's actually in their food.

Scan the barcode on a bag, can or pouch. Zevli reads the label and tells you what's in it, in plain English. It doesn't tell you what to buy. It tells you what's in the bag, and leaves the choice with you.

A score out of 100FREE

Worked out from the food's own label by fixed rules, the same for every food it scores. Tap it to see how it's worked out.

Ingredients, explainedFREE

The full list, grouped by what each one does. Tap any ingredient to see what it is and why it's there.

How it comparesFREE

Where each figure sits among similar foods. Not better or worse, just lower, about average or higher.

Zevli covers dog and cat foods sold by major pet retailers, supermarkets and department stores, online and in store: complete foods, treats, toppers and vet diets, with new products added all the time. Complete foods are scored. Other types are recorded and explained.
