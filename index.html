How it works

Scan it. Read it. Decide for yourself.

Zevli doesn't tell you what to buy. It tells you what's in the bag and where it sits, and leaves the choice where it belongs.

A score out of 100

Worked out from the food's own label by fixed rules, the same for every food. Tap it and Zevli shows how a score is worked out.

Ingredients, explained

The full list in label order, as printed. With Plus, tap an ingredient to see what it is and why it's there.

How it compares

With Plus, where each figure sits among similar foods. Not better or worse, just higher, lower, or in the middle.
