Your community. Your voice.

We're not asking you to become a content creator.

You know your members better than we do. We provide the finished content and suggested copy. You choose the available creative that best suits your community.

What kind of groups can apply?

We're initially looking for active Australian Facebook communities built around dogs, cats, breeds, pet ownership, pet food and related pet interests. Your group should primarily serve people in Australia.

How does payment work?

Payment is a fixed amount agreed before the placement begins. It is not calculated from installs, views or CPM.

Does my community need to be Australian?

Yes. This page is for the Australian campaign. Your Facebook group should primarily serve an Australian pet-owner audience. Other Zevli markets will have their own regional partner programs.

What do I charge?

You can choose Fixed fee per post up to an agreed campaign limit, or discuss a fixed fee for an agreed campaign. Payment terms are confirmed before you post.

Do I need to pay for ads?

No. You do not buy advertising. The program is about introducing Zevli organically to the community you've already built.

Do I need to create content?

No. Zevli supplies finished video and static creatives plus suggested copy. You choose from the content available to your placement.

Do I get access to Zevli?

Yes. Approved partners can receive Zevli access so you understand the product you are sharing with your community.

What if I already charge for advertising?

Tell us. Include your normal sponsored-post rate and, if you have one, your usual multi-post package. We can negotiate the number of placements and total fixed fee.

What does a fixed campaign look like?

It could be one post or a package such as one video plus one static post. The exact creative, number of posts, fee and timing are agreed before anything goes live.
