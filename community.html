Your community. Your voice.

We're not asking you to become an influencer.

You know your members better than we do. We provide the campaign and tools. You decide how best to introduce Zevli to your community while keeping it authentic.

What kind of groups can apply?

Active Facebook communities for dog or cat owners, including general ownership, breeds and pet care, from any country. We assess each group on its audience and suitability.

How much will I earn?

You can nominate your fee in your preferred currency. If you have never advertised or are unsure what to charge, ask Zevli to make an offer. We agree a fixed fee before the campaign begins.

Are there any sales or install targets?

No. Payment is based on completing the agreed placements, not on installs, sales or subscriptions.

Do I need to pay for ads?

No. You publish the agreed content in your existing Facebook group.

Do I need to create videos?

No. Choose four ready-to-post videos from our private content library. We supply captions and a discussion prompt.

Do I get access to Zevli?

Yes. Approved partners receive complimentary Zevli access so they can understand the app they are sharing.

What is the administrator-led discussion?

One genuine community conversation about pet care or Zevli, using a suggested prompt. Keep the discussion open to honest feedback and disclose the paid partnership.

How do I get paid?

Payment method, currency and timing are agreed in writing before you publish. There is no automatic renewal.

Can I apply from anywhere?

Yes. We welcome applications globally and assess the location and activity of your members, not just where you live.

Do I need to disclose the partnership?

Yes. Clearly identify sponsored posts and discussions as paid partnerships. We provide suggested disclosure wording.

What if I have never advertised before?

No previous advertising experience is needed. Choose the option asking Zevli to make an offer. We will review your audience and discuss a suitable fixed fee before you commit.
