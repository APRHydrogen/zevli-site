Content can include

  • Voiceover or faceless content — camera confidence isn't mandatory

Quick answers

Do I need followers?

No. We're specifically interested in finding people with creative ability, not simply renting established audiences.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Great pet footage, voiceovers, editing, app demonstrations and other formats can work.

Are payments in USD?

Yes. Creator challenge and performance payments are calculated in US dollars.

Do views from all four platforms add together?

No. For each approved video, only the highest eligible view count from a single platform is used for the performance calculation.

Is everyone accepted?

No. Applications and challenge work are reviewed for quality and fit. Ongoing participation is by invitation.
